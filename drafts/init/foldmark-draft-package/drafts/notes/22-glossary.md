# Glossary

- **Document:** semantic content and metadata independent of output medium.
- **Print profile:** physical page/surface geometry, regions and helper marks.
- **Marker:** fold, hole, cut, safe, bleed, address/stamp or custom geometry.
- **Render plan:** resolved, deterministic representation used by a renderer.
- **Surface:** one printable side, such as postcard front or back.
- **Export target:** print, PDF, Markdown, HTML, email, EML, image or ODT.
- **Capability:** optional lazy-loaded integration activated through explicit consent.
- **Sender profile:** reusable sender address, contact and letterhead defaults.
- **Graphical signature:** image of handwriting, not a cryptographic/legal qualified signature.
- **Local-first:** user data and primary workflows operate locally in the browser.
