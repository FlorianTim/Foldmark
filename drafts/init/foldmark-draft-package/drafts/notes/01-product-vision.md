# Product vision

## Vision statement

Foldmark is a privacy-friendly, local-first web application that turns structured text into reliable physical and digital correspondence. Users can write once and render the same content for different paper sizes, envelopes, cards, photographs and email workflows.

## Differentiator

The main feature is a configurable print-profile and marker engine. It must support physical helper geometry such as:

- fold marks
- hole/punch marks
- cut marks
- safe areas
- bleed
- address windows
- stamp areas
- separators and guides
- front/back or duplex surfaces

## Product principles

1. **Local first:** normal use works offline after loading the app.
2. **Privacy by default:** no unrequested third-party network calls.
3. **Open formats:** Markdown/YAML and JSON are primary portable formats.
4. **Print accuracy:** physical units are first-class domain data.
5. **Progressive capabilities:** connectors and complex exporters are optional.
6. **Explainability:** warnings explain why a profile or export is invalid.
7. **Accessibility:** keyboard and screen-reader operation are required.
8. **Agent-maintainable:** OpenSpec, arc42, ADRs and diagrams evolve with the code.

## Intended users

- private users writing formal letters
- freelancers and small businesses maintaining sender identities and letterheads
- clubs and associations producing recurring correspondence
- users creating postcards or cards with front/back layouts
- privacy-conscious users avoiding cloud office suites
- developers who want reusable, open document formats
