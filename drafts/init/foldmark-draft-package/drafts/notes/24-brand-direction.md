# Brand direction

## Working brand

**Foldmark** combines a physical paper fold with a precise mark. The supplied logo uses a folded page corner and horizontal guide marks.

## Tone

- reliable and technical, not bureaucratic
- calm, privacy-conscious and approachable
- paper/print heritage with modern local-first software

## Colors

- Deep teal: `#174A5B`
- Bright teal: `#2F7D8C`
- Fold amber: `#E3A53B`
- Paper: `#FAF8F2`
- Ink: `#182126`

## Typography

Use a system font stack by default. Do not load Google Fonts. A locally packaged open font may be considered only after license and privacy review.

## Icon style

Original 24×24 SVG line icons, rounded line caps, two-pixel nominal stroke, currentColor, no external font/icon dependency.
